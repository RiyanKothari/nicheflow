Build the Invoicing module at route "/invoices". Use the authenticated layout.

HEADER:
- Page title: "Invoices"
- Stats row: Total Revenue (this month), Outstanding Amount, Paid this month, Overdue count
- Right: Filter by status + Date range + "+ New Invoice" accent button

INVOICE LIST:
- Table: Invoice #, Client, Amount, Status, Due Date, Actions
- Status badges with colors: Draft (gray), Sent (blue), Viewed (purple), Paid (teal/green), Overdue (red), Cancelled (gray strikethrough)
- Overdue invoices shown with red row highlight and "X days overdue" badge
- Sort by date, amount, status, client
- Filter by status, client, date range
- Search by invoice number or client name
- Clicking an invoice opens the invoice detail/preview

NEW INVOICE FORM (slide-in panel):
- Client selector — searchable dropdown
- Invoice number — auto-generated (INV-001, INV-002...) but editable
- Issue date + Due date (smart defaults: 7/15/30 days or custom)
- Line items table:
  · Each row: Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Total (auto-calculated)
  · "+ Add line item" button
  · Remove row button
  · Niche-specific service suggestions autofill in description dropdown
- Subtotal (auto-calculated)
- Discount field — fixed (₹) or percentage (%)
- Tax field — GST percentage, shows calculated amount
- Total (auto-calculated, large and prominent)
- Notes/terms textarea
- "Save as Draft" + "Save & Send" buttons

INVOICE PREVIEW PAGE at "/invoices/:id":
- Clean, professional invoice layout — looks like a real invoice

- Business name, logo, address top left

- Invoice number, dates
- Line items table

- Notes/terms section

PAYMENT TRACKING:

BACKEND:
- GET /api/invoices — all invoices with filters
